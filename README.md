# Wifey Homecare Hub

Design a modern, friendly homepage for an Australian home-help brand “Hire a Wifey” (Hervey Bay). Use the brand colors – a warm pink (sophisticated blush) and dark charcoal, with white clean accents. The site must look premium, professional and trustworthy, yet also approachable and slightly cheeky. The hero section should have a high-quality background image of a cheerful uniformed cleaner (with a subtle Hire a Wifey logo on her outfit) doing a home task (e.g. cleaning or cooking), overlaid with the tagline “We Take Care of Home”. Below the tagline include supporting text like “Reclaim your free time – we handle cleaning, laundry & more.” Two prominent buttons should appear: a pink “Book a Wifey” CTA (links to booking page) and a border-button “Ask a Question” that scrolls to the contact form. Ensure the CTA stands out (rounded corners, white text on pink). Below the hero, create a mobile-first layout listing services: use six icons or images in two columns labeled Cleaning, Laundry, Ironing, Bed-making, Organising, Meal Prep, each with a tiny explanatory caption. Include a section titled “NDIS & Veterans” with two icon badges and text “We proudly support NDIS and DVA clients” (use wheelchair and badge icons). Then add a Hervey Bay Service Area section: include a small map graphic or pin image of Hervey Bay and the text “Serving all of Hervey Bay and surrounds.” Next, show a Reviews slider or grid with two customer quotes and star ratings (“★★★★★”). After that, add an FAQ accordion section answering common queries (FAQ headings only). Then a recruitment blurb with a button “Become a Wifey” linking to careers. Finally, include a compact Contact form (fields: Name, Email, Message) next to a small Google Map embed. All text should be crisp and benefit-focused. Use clear H1/H2 hierarchies: H1 on hero, H2 for sections (e.g. “Our Services”, “Reviews”). Choose web-safe fonts like Montserrat for headings and Open Sans for body. Make navigation minimal (logo plus a hamburger or sticky “Book Now” footer on mobile). The design must be fully responsive: on mobile, stack content vertically and include a fixed bottom “Book Now” bar. Keep imagery real and positive – no cartoon characters. Ensure white space around sections (ample padding). The pink buttons (#E75480) and icons can repeat accent color.
